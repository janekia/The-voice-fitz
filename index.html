About Me

Who am I?

"I am who I’ve always aspired to be — an artist at heart, using my voice to connect with people through emotion and meaning. Voice acting has always been my passion, ever since it expanded my world growing up. Whether through humor or raw emotional depth, it gave me new perspectives and helped me see beyond my own experience. Now, I strive to give that same feeling to others through my work."
